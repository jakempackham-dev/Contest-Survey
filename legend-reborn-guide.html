How to play
Fast Track

The game runs on Chapters — and you're already behind.

Legend Reborn's launch content runs on Chapters — regional story beats that build toward a bigger confrontation, server by server. Clear them and you get real rewards: Artifacts, new Feats, gold, and a stake in something bigger. Every Chapter your server clears as a community pushes the whole server's Server Level up.

When the Server Level rises, everything gets better. Shops stock gear they didn't carry last week. Monsters get tougher — and more worth fighting. Magic gets louder. Spells that were pushing your limits get safer to cast. It's not abstract number-go-up. It's a world that visibly grows because you and everyone else earned it.

Full Brief — Combat & Encounter Modes

Legend Reborn runs on the Contest system — a fast, decisive combat engine where two sides roll dice and the higher number wins. No hit points that disappear one at a time. No death spirals. Fights are sharp, outcomes matter, and every character has something specific they're built to do.

Duel
1v1 — alternating turns, full intensity. The purest test of a character build. You go, they go, one of you stops first. Great for settling disputes, boss phases, and the moments that define rivalries.
Skirmish
Party vs party — side-based, tactical. Your bread and butter. Your party acts, their side acts. Positioning matters, covering your allies matters, and the right ability at the right moment can swing everything.
Hunt
Chase and pursuit — instinct over stats. Rock-paper-scissors logic at its core: pursue, evade, or ambush. Speed builds and tracker archetypes shine here. The monster either gets away or it doesn't.
Battle
Army vs army — macro and micro. The biggest scale in the game. You control units at the strategic level and individual characters at the tactical level simultaneously. Guild conquests, province wars, and server-defining confrontations live here.

Every character has a Role (what they are in a fight), an Archetype (how they do it), Stats (which dice they roll), Feats (edges they've earned), a Flaw (what makes them human), and a Special Move — the thing they do when everything is on the line and holding back is no longer an option.

Tenacity is the resource that makes this system feel alive. You earn it on failure. You spend it to tip rolls in your favor. The longer a fight goes badly, the more dangerous you become. The party that's been losing all night is the party you do not want to corner.

Your party
Fast Track

You're not starting alone.

You're logging in as a group. That's unusual — most Reborn arrive solo, find their footing, maybe fall in with other players over time. Your party is already formed before you've taken your first step in Concordia. That's an advantage. Use it.

Your characters don't know each other yet — but they're arriving at the same time, in the same city, at the same Server Level. How they meet, why they stay together, and what they're willing to do for each other is the first story you'll tell.

Economy
Fast Track

Gear up. The market is already moving.

Callista has a full player-driven economy — shops, black markets, player trading, and crafted items that actually matter. What's available depends on where you are and what your server has earned. Higher Server Level means better stock. Better stock means better fights. Better fights mean more gold.

There are armories, blade smiths, fletchers, alchemists, stables, and stranger things in every major city. Find the right vendor for your build and check back as the server grows.

Full Brief — Shop Grades & Store Types

Every store type exists at multiple quality grades. The grade determines what's on the shelves, how powerful it is, and how rare it is to find. You'll start with access to Common shops in Concordia — the higher tiers unlock as the server levels and new cities open up.

Grade What's available Access
Common Standard gear, basic components, everyday supplies. Functional and available everywhere. What every new Reborn starts with. All cities
Quality Noticeably better materials, tighter craftsmanship. You'll feel the difference in a fight. Found in larger cities and trade hubs. Major cities
Superb Named materials, rare components, items with properties that don't show up in Common or Quality stock. Worth traveling for. Server Level 2+
Mastercraft The best in the world. One Mastercraft version of each store type exists per server — find it, and you've found something most players never will. These items have names. One per server

Before you log in
The Character Scan
Fast Track + Full Brief — read this section regardless

Before Session Zero, you'll receive a link. When you open it, Legend Reborn's onboarding AI will conduct your character scan — the same system the game uses for every new Reborn. Answer honestly. The game is paying attention.

This is not a quiz with right answers. It's a conversation. The AI adapts to what you say — every question is built from your previous answer. The more honest you are, the more accurate your character will be. A character built from who you actually are is more interesting than a character built from who you think you should be.

Important

This survey generates your actual character sheet. Your Role, Archetype, Stats, Feats, Flaw, Equipment, and Special Move all come from this conversation. Your GM receives a private brief based on your answers. Take it seriously — but don't overthink it. The best results come from gut responses, not optimized ones.

What the scan covers:

Phase 1 Identity Scan. Who are you in real life? Physical presence, instincts under pressure, what you're genuinely good at. The game is reading your biometrics — answer as yourself, not as a character concept.
Phase 2 Combat & Instinct. What do you default to in games like Skyrim or Elden Ring? Do you want to absorb punishment or deal it? Surgical precision or hit everything in the room? This determines your Role and weapon loadout.
Phase 3 Reference Pull. What characters from any medium — anime, games, books, film — resonate with you and why? The game cross-references your cultural data to triangulate your Archetype. Push past surface answers.
Phase 4 The Wound. The game needs to understand your vulnerabilities to build your resistances. This reveals your Flaw — not by asking for it directly, but by listening for the pattern. Be honest here.
Phase 5 Ability Workshop. Your three Abilities and Special Move get proposed and workshopped. The Special Move name and description are yours to shape — this is the one thing you build together. Name it something worth shouting.

When the scan is complete, your character sheet generates automatically and your GM receives a private brief. You'll see everything except the GM brief — that's theirs. Read your herald text carefully. It knows something about you.

What to expect
Fast Track

This is a launch-week MMO campaign.

Fresh world. Fresh stakes. Real excitement in the air. A Story Line that's genuinely fun to chase — big bosses, good loot, real character growth. Play into that. Get excited about your Chapter clears. Pick a direction and lean into it. Rival other players if that's your thing, or don't, and just enjoy the ride.

Your backstory should fit wherever you're starting: a Mercian provincial upbringing, a hardened Felcho survivor, a Brandia-born monster hunter's apprentice, or an outsider who just arrived and hasn't picked a side yet. All valid. All already have hooks waiting.

One thing worth knowing

The world reacts to what you do. NPCs remember. Factions shift. The border dispute in Ashford Province is already pulling in other players — and the outcome of Chapter 1 will affect what Chapter 2 looks like for your entire server. Your decisions matter beyond your own story.

Everything else — you'll find out as you go.

It should feel less like a build you optimized and more like a version of yourself the game recognized before you did.
